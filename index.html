Independent Applied Research · Multibody Dynamics

Researching robust simulation for complex mechanical systems

ManifoldsX is an independent applied-research project exploring numerical methods and browser-based workflows for constrained mechanisms, robotics, contact-rich dynamics, and flexible multibody systems.

Research Focus

Questions driving the work

The project investigates practical numerical methods that make complex mechanism simulation more transparent, inspectable, and reproducible.

⛓️

Constrained Multibody Dynamics

Implicit, constraint-based formulations for closed-loop mechanisms, long kinematic chains, and stiff mechanical systems.

🤖

Parallel Mechanisms

Modeling and actuation studies for Stewart platforms, hexapods, and multi-stage robotic mechanisms.

💥

Contact & Impact

Contact models and numerical strategies for stable impact, compliant contact, and contact-rich motion studies.

∇

Sensitivity & Optimization

Analytical model structure intended to support parameter studies, calibration, and future gradient-based workflows.

Methods & Prototypes

Current technical direction

Implicit INDEX-3 DAE Formulation

Position-level constraint formulations and Newton iterations for stiff, closed-loop mechanical systems.

Sensitivity-Oriented Architecture

Analytical kinematics and mechanics organized to support repeatable parameter studies and future derivative-based research.

Mechanism Primitives

Joints, prescribed motions, forces, compliant elements, and prototype contact models for controlled mechanism experiments.

Browser-Based Research Interface

A visual environment for building test models, inspecting states, running experiments, and reviewing solver output.

Contact Numerics

Ongoing work on contact detection, compliant impact, and future friction formulations for contact-rich motion studies.

C++ Experimental Core

A C++17 solver core supporting controlled benchmarks, numerical diagnostics, and constrained-dynamics experiments.

Experimental Studies

Mechanism and solver studies

Selected model studies from the current research prototype. These demonstrations are illustrative, not validation against a physical test rig.

Two-Stage Stewart Platform Study

A coordinated parallel mechanism used to study multiple driven constraints and actuator trajectories.

100-Link Constraint Propagation Study

A high-DOF chain used to examine constraint propagation, stability, and repeatable nonlinear motion.

Single-Stage Stewart Platform Benchmark

A six-leg parallel mechanism used to examine closed-loop constraint behavior under prescribed actuator motion.
