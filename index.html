Physics-based dynamics

Accurate, differentiable simulation for robots and machines.

A dynamics solver for engineers who design robots, parallel platforms and machines with flexible parts. It computes motion and loads, and how both change with your design parameters. Available today through simulation pilots.

Flexible interaction

Accurate loads

Joint and actuator loads through the whole duty cycle. What does each actuator have to carry, and with what margin?

The question: when two arms move a cotton sheet, how does the sheet lag, fold and ripple? Arms and cloth are solved as one model, so the sheet’s weight and drag act on the grippers and arm joints. Rendered from the solver output.

The question: how does a loose material flow and settle? About 15,000 grains, each with elastic contact, friction and rolling resistance. This is the groundwork for buckets, grippers and wheels working in sand or powder.

Calibrate a model from measured motion

A pendulum hangs from a sliding block on a spring of unknown stiffness. We start from a guess 13 % too low. Each run returns the pendulum tip’s path and its derivative with respect to stiffness, computed analytically in the same run, and that derivative gives the next estimate. After three runs the model matches the measured path to the noise level, and the stiffness comes back as 226 N/m, within 2×10−5 N/m of the true value.

Long-run accuracy: ten classical problems, each run for 1,000 seconds against an exact answer. Read the paper.

How it works

1

Build the model

Parts, joints, springs, bushings and motions, in ManifoldsX Studio (a browser editor) or as a JSON file.

2

Solve

Fixed or error-controlled steps, with every constraint held to its tolerance.

3

Inspect and report

Play back the motion; joint and actuator loads and parameter sensitivities go into the report.
