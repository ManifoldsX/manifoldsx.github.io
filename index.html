Technology

What powers manifoldsx

Implicit DAE Solver

INDEX-3 constrained dynamics with HHT-α integration for stiff, high-speed mechanical systems.

Analytical Jacobians

Core joints, forces, and contact models use analytical derivatives to improve Newton convergence and robustness.

Closed-Chain Mechanisms

Parallel mechanisms, Stewart platforms, four-bar linkages, and constrained robotic systems with tight constraint enforcement.

Differentiation-Ready Physics

An analytical solver architecture designed for optimization, sensitivity analysis, and future gradient-based workflows.

Flexible Multibody Systems

Rigid and flexible components can be coupled in one simulation architecture for advanced mechanism and structure studies.

Browser-Based Workflow

Build, inspect, solve, animate, and record models using a modern 3D interface designed for engineering workflows.

Contact & Impact

Hunt-Crossley compliant contact with analytical tangent stiffness for contact-rich motion and impact studies.

C++ Solver Core

A high-performance solver core written from scratch in C++ for fast implicit stepping and engineering-scale models.
