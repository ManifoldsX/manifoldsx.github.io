Multibody simulation

Accurate motion and loads for robots and machines.

ManifoldsX is a multibody solver for engineers who design robots, parallel platforms and machines with flexible parts. It computes the motion, the loads in every joint and actuator, and how both change with your design parameters. Today it is offered through simulation pilots: bring one mechanism and one question, and get a report you can check.

Coordinated motion

Many actuators driving one mechanism, with closed loops that stay closed. Does the platform go exactly where the legs put it?

Flexible interaction

Machines that carry or contain soft parts: fabric, flexible links, beams. How does the part deform as the machine moves it?

Accurate loads

Joint and actuator loads through the whole duty cycle. What does each actuator have to carry, and with what margin?

The question: when two arms move a cotton sheet, how does the sheet lag, fold and ripple? Arms and cloth are one model, so the grippers carry the sheet’s real load. Rendered from the solver output.

How it works

1

Build the model

Parts, joints, springs, bushings and motions, in Studio or as a JSON file.

2

Solve

Fixed or error-controlled steps, with every constraint held to its tolerance.

3

Inspect and report

Play back the motion; joint and actuator loads and parameter sensitivities go into the report.
