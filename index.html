Closed-loop accuracy

Two stacked Stewart platforms driven by twelve actuators. Position and orientation are compared with an independent forward-kinematics solution over an 18-second simulation.

Model
Two stacked Stewart platforms, 12 driven legs, 18 s
Reference
Exact forward kinematics from the leg lengths
Result
Both platforms within 10−9 m in position and 2×10−9 rad in orientation of the exact pose
Speed
About 12× real time on one core of a 2.1 GHz Intel Xeon
Settings
HHT index-3, error control at 10−5, steps up to 3 ms

Differentiable physics

Analytic gradients turn a simulation into a calibration tool. From one 8-second tracker recording, they identify all seven joint stiffnesses of a robot arm. The calibrated model then predicts the tool on a new motion to within 2 µm.
