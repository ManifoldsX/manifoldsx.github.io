Benchmark, updated 23 October 2026

Two-stage Stewart platform, checked against forward kinematics

The platform is driven by its 12 leg lengths for 18 seconds. With every joint closed, each platform's pose is pure geometry, so the exact answer is known without running any dynamics. ManifoldsX and a MuJoCo model of the same mechanism are scored against it.

Result

Over the full run, ManifoldsX keeps the top platform within a micrometre of the exact pose, at about 13 times real time on one core. MuJoCo at a similar speed is about a tenth of a millimetre off, and tens of millimetres off with its default settings.

One model with prescribed motion. It says nothing about contact or about running many environments in parallel, which is what MuJoCo is designed for.
