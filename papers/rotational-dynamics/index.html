Technical report

Three studies in rotational dynamics

A gimballed gyroscope, a spinning top and a tumbling body: three familiar motions, checked against mechanics.

Each case was simulated for 12 seconds at fixed steps of 1, 0.5 and 0.25 ms. All nine runs completed without retries or step cuts. The animations use the 1 ms runs.

Report (PDF, 5 pages)

Gimballed gyroscope

The rotor sits inside two freely moving gimbals. It starts spinning while the whole assembly is given an initial rotation about the outer hinge, and the spinning rotor couples the motion of the two gimbals.

What was checked

Energy, angular momentum about the outer hinge and the rotor's spin stay constant. Halving the step barely changes the motion.

Spinning top

The top spins about its own axis while that axis travels around the vertical. The initial motion is set for steady precession, so the tilt stays constant.

What was checked

The orientation matches the exact steady-precession solution, and the error falls quickly as the step shrinks.

Tumbling body

A free body starts spinning almost exactly about its intermediate axis of inertia. A small disturbance grows until the body flips, and the flip repeats without any external torque.

What was checked

All four flips in 12 seconds match the analytical solution.
